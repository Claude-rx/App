How it started

Born in a small studio.

Born from a simple yet powerful idea — that beauty should be a celebration, not a standard — Skinofab began as a small studio with one mission: to redefine how the world sees cosmetics. What started with a single formula grew into a focused line for skin after 45: an adaptive peptide foundation, an adaptive foundation stick, a PDRN plumping balm, an EGF + NAD+ firming serum and a lymphatic brush.

Every product is thoughtfully crafted, combining high-performance formulas with skin-loving ingredients — designed for real people and real routines.
