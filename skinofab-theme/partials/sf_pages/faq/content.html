Results & Usage

Results are visible immediately — our adaptive pigment formula visibly adjusts to your skin tone within seconds of application. In our clinical study, 88% of users reported improved skin texture after just 2 weeks of daily wear. Our 8-week independent customer panel showed 98% of users found their blemishes became invisible.

Follow these simple steps for a flawless finish:

  1. Create the Perfect Canvas: Start with moisturized skin. Apply your favorite primer or hydrating cream to create an even base.
  2. Build Your Base: Dispense one pump onto the back of your hand. Dot the foundation on your forehead, cheeks, nose, and chin. Blend outward from the center using circular motions for a naturally soft, medium coverage finish.
  3. Customize Your Coverage: Want more coverage? Layer a second application on any areas that need extra attention — blemishes, redness, or discoloration — and blend seamlessly.
  4. Lock It In: Brighten under-eyes with your favorite concealer, then lightly dust translucent powder across your T-zone to control shine and keep your look fresh all day.
