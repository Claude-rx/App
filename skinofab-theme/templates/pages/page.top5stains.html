The top 5 lip stains of 2026

If you’ve scrolled TikTok recently, you’ve probably noticed everyone talking about lip stains. From quick “apply and peel” tutorials to all-day wear tests, lip stains have officially taken over. But with so many brands claiming to have the “perfect” formula, how do you know which ones are actually worth it?

What is a lip stain?

A lip stain is a lightweight lip product designed to give your lips long-lasting color without the heavy, waxy feel of traditional lipsticks. Unlike glosses or creams that sit on the surface, stains sink into the lips, leaving behind a tint that looks more natural and usually lasts much longer.


They’re ideal for anyone who wants color that doesn’t transfer, fade after a few sips of coffee, or need constant reapplication.
