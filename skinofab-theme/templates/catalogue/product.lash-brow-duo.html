Eyebrow Serum

  1. Remove your makeup with an oil-free makeup remover or cleanser
  2. Apply a thin layer across your entire eyebrow line. One dip per eyebrow.
  3. Wait until the serum has fully dried before applying any additional products
  4. Allow the serum to work overnight

Eyelash Serum

  1. Remove your makeup with an oil-free makeup remover or cleanser
  2. Apply a thin layer on your eyelashes. One dip total for both eyelashes.
  3. Wait until the serum has fully dried before applying any additional products
  4. Allow the serum to work overnight
