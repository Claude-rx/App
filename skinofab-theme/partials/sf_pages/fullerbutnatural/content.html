OUR INGREDIENTS

We care about your lips

At Skinofab, we care about more than just instant results—we care about your lips. Our Extreme Lip Plumper blends safe, naturally-derived ingredients like Hyaluronic Acid, Squalane, and Collagen to hydrate, smooth, and enhance volume without irritation. It’s a clean formula that plumps with care.

Hyaluronic Acid is a naturally occurring substance in the body known for its incredible ability to retain moisture. In our Extreme Lip Plumper, it works by drawing in water to the lips, creating an instant plumping effect while keeping them hydrated and smooth throughout the day. This helps reduce the appearance of fine lines and gives your lips a fuller, more youthful look—naturally.

Squalane is a plant-derived ingredient that mimics your skin’s natural oils, making it incredibly effective at locking in moisture without feeling greasy. In our formula, it conditions and softens the lips, leaving them silky-smooth and protected against dryness. Squalane is also rich in antioxidants, making it a gentle yet powerful way to nourish your lips with every use.

Collagen is the protein responsible for maintaining skin’s firmness and elasticity, but as we age, our natural collagen levels decline. That’s why we’ve added collagen to help support structure and volume in the lips. It works to restore plumpness, improve texture, and promote a naturally fuller look over time.
