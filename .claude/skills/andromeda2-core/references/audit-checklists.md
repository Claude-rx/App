## 1. Ad Review Bible — 15 reasons an ad fails

Name the reason(s) for every loser; it turns a dead ad into a learning.

| # | Reason | What it looks like | Fix direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Editing | Too disruptive or wrong style for the audience; text too fast/slow | Match pace and style to the side; text readable at a glance |
| 2 | Product focus | No focal point; too much text; unclear what is sold | One message, one focal subject, product visible |
| 3 | Benefits | Problem isn't a real problem for them; benefit doesn't serve their desire; not believable | Re-research; pick the benefit that serves the desire |
| 4 | Text length | Lines long-winded, not punchy; overwhelming | Cut; read it out loud |
| 5 | Visual hook | Doesn't stop the scroll | Study creators your buyers watch; hands/close-up/emotion/demo in 0-2 s |
| 6 | Text hook | Headline doesn't intrigue or pull into the rest | Specific, curiosity + call-out of the side |
| 7 | Belief | Doesn't make them believe it will work for *them* | Proof matched to that side's objection; mechanism |
| 8 | Identity | People, scenery, words don't match the identity they buy into | Cast and set for the identity (luxury ≠ trailer park) |
| 9 | Desire | No desire or the wrong desire (selling a Mustang as eco-friendly) | Show the product accomplishing the desire: in action, day-1 and over-time benefits, a test they can do |
| 10 | Awareness | Wrong awareness level; aware-level ads don't scale | Shift toward problem-aware / unaware for scale |
| 11 | Mass objection | Skepticism not handled ("it's a scam") | Name and answer the main objection inside the ad |
| 12 | Product utility | How it works isn't clear | Feature, describe or name the mechanism |
| 13 | Product value | Seems expensive | Compare against a pricier way to get the same desire (silk vs regular pajamas; home laser vs clinic) |
| 14 | Exhausted visuals | Market has seen it too long | Fresh visuals, new formats |
| 15 | Exhausted messaging | Same message as you and competitors for too long | New angle, mechanism or education point |

## 2. Pre-brief questions for any ad

- Do the first 1-2 seconds hook the RIGHT person and keep them?
- Would your customers *want* to see this? Does it feel native to the platform? Does it NOT look like an ad?
- Does the copy create a clear picture in the mind?
- Does it label who it is for (call-out of the side)?
- Would it make someone smile or laugh? Is it relatable? Does it feel authentic?
- Which learnings from previous winners are applied?
- Does it educate creatively on how to reach the core desire? Is the claim escalated in a clear, relatable way?
- Does it tell an interesting story, entertain, or educate with a believable new mechanism?
- Does it separate the brand from competitors?

## 3. Pellegrini's 5-question script audit

1. **Who are we talking to?** A person with a belief and a story — or just "you"?
2. **Mood × Intensity?** Which zone (COZY, JOY, FATIGUE, PANIC) — and is it chosen or accidental ("pharma brochure tone")?
3. **Awareness stage?** Clear, or does nothing say whether they tried four products or just discovered the problem?
4. **Self-concept?** Actual, ideal or ought — is the version they want to become named?
5. **How do we prove it?** Named mechanism and anchored proof — or "turmeric, anti-inflammatory, 100% natural, expert
   approved", the claims of 500 brands?
Then the three fixes: desire = the life regained (not the feature); name the cause and the solution (UMP/UMS, linked in 2 s);
anchor claims with numbers, names and timeframes.

## 4. Account audit — the eight Andromeda 2 requirements

For each line: evidence (screenshot/number) → verdict → impact on the goal metrics → fix → owner skill.

**R1 one side per ad / R4 diversity of intent**
- How many distinct concepts (side × template) are live? How many avatars? Do several ads ask the same question?
- Concept vs iteration labels exist? Hit rate by creative category tracked?

**R2 rooms**
- Placement breakdown (sorted by spend): do FB/IG Feed, Stories and Reels all carry spend? A top-six placement near 1% =
  missing format (usually vertical video).
- 9:16 + 1:1 on every ad? Captions/text for sound-off?

**R3 specificity**
- Sample 10 top-spend ads: count floating claims vs anchored proof; named mechanism present?

**R5 forced spend**
- Share of budget in ABO tests, minimums, scale campaigns. Minimums older than 7 days? Floors on fatiguing ads?

**R6 competitive structure**
- One prospecting pool where new creative competes? New rounds as new packs (not injected)? Purchasers excluded from
  prospecting and targeted in retention (≤ 10%)? Spend concentration on top ads (5-10 ads ≈ 60-70% is normal)?

**R7 signal**
- Purchase optimisation; pixel + CAPI; EMQ ≥ 8.4; server purchases vs store orders; learning-phase share; recent resets of
  winners; anything rewriting live URLs/UTMs; attribution settings consistent.

**R8 volume with context**
- New ads/week vs 1 per $10k monthly spend; iteration vs new-concept mix vs spend tier; research artefacts (side cards,
  phrase bank) actually used in briefs?

**Economics and measurement**
- King goal defined from a profit model? MER vs Meta ROAS gap? Incremental vs reported by audience segment?
- Engaged share of prospecting spend (10-25% normal); frequency by segment vs caps.
- Kill discipline: pauses on < 7 days of data? Top spender paused while account on target?

| Requirement | Score 0-2 | Evidence | Impact on CPM / CTR / hook / hold / CPC / CPP / ROAS |

| Ad | Concept/iteration | Side | Scorecard /20 | Failure reasons (#) | Weakest stage (a2) | One change |
