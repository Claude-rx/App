# Static batch — Nestwell Lift Bed (made-up brand) — 2026-11-18

**Assumptions & placeholders:** price $149 [P]; offer = free shipping, 100-night trial, second bed 40% off [P]; 90-day
compression test result "keeps hips ~3 in off the floor" [P — needs the test report]; review rating 4.8 from 2,000+ reviews [P];
founder's dog Juno, 13 [P — founder must confirm the story]; monthly spend $60k [P] → ~6 new ads/week (1 per $10k).
Category: pet product — no medical claims (we say "sleeps through", not "treats arthritis").

**Sides**
- **A01 — Dana, whose 12-year-old lab paces at 2 a.m.** Moment: woken by nails on the floor; he circles, then lies on the cold
  kitchen tile. Says: "I just want him comfortable." Believes: "he's just old". Desire: both of them sleeping through; him
  stretched out like he used to. Fear: his last years being uncomfortable. Self: actual "I'm a good dog mom" / ought "I owe
  him comfort after 12 years". Awareness: problem aware. Zones: FATIGUE, COZY.
- **A02 — Rob, three beds later.** Bought 3 "orthopedic" beds in 2 years ($60-90 each [P]); each went flat; the dog sleeps on
  the floor next to an expensive pancake. Enemy: foam that flattens. Says: "I'm not falling for 'orthopedic' again."
  Awareness: solution aware, sophistication 4 (every bed claims "orthopedic"). Zones: FATIGUE → COZY.

**Mechanism:** UMP "the Pancake Effect" — single-layer foam compresses under a big dog's hips within months, so hips and elbows
end up on the hard floor and he avoids the bed. UMS "Two-Layer Lift" — a 4-in high-density base + a 2-in top layer that springs
back every morning [P]. Link: flat bed → joints on the floor → he leaves it; lift layer → he stays → you both sleep.

**Batch goal:** new concepts (angle test across two sides) · 6 concepts, 9 ads · mix 5 TOF (S1, S2, S5) / 4 BOF (S3, S4, S6) ads · two packs over two weeks.

## Per-static specs

### S1 — C01 · A01 Dana-2am × Problem–solution split — concept
- **Question it answers:** "Why does he pick the cold tile over his bed?"
- **Side & moment:** Dana, 2 a.m., woken by nails on the floor · Awareness problem · Zone FATIGUE · Self ought · Lever avatar.
- **On-image copy:** L1 "2 a.m. Kitchen tile. Again." · L2 "Old dogs leave beds that have gone flat. Give him one that
  doesn't." · L3 chip "100-night trial".
- **Visual direction:** focal subject = the dog (same dog both halves). Left: dim, cool blue moonlight, grey-muzzled black lab
  curled on cream tiles, eyes open, a flat worn bed out of focus behind. Right: warm morning light, the same dog fully stretched,
  asleep on the Nestwell bed. Casting = the side's dog (large, senior). Type: plain sentence case, white on the dark half.
- **1:1:** two equal halves, thin white divider; L1 across the top fifth spanning both halves; L2 under the right half; chip
  bottom-right. **9:16:** L1 at 16-24% height; night image 24-45%; morning image 45-65%; bottom 35% = floor of the morning scene,
  no text; chip at ~60%.
- **AI image prompt (1:1):**
  ```
  Square 1:1 photo-realistic split image, two equal halves, thin white vertical divider. LEFT: a dim kitchen at 2 a.m., cool blue
  moonlight from a window, an old black Labrador with a grey muzzle (about 12 years old) lying curled on cream ceramic floor
  tiles, eyes open, slightly tense; a flat, worn grey dog bed out of focus in the background. RIGHT: the same dog in soft warm
  morning light, fully stretched out and asleep on the Nestwell Lift Bed exactly as in reference image 1 (rectangular, 36 x 28 x
  6 in, sage-green zip cover, cream piping, fabric tag on the front-left corner facing camera), bed flat on a light wooden floor,
  camera at dog eye level, bed in the same position and angle as the reference. Keep the top fifth of both halves plain wall for
  a headline. Natural phone-camera look. No text, no other logos, one dog only, realistic paws and fur.
  ```
  References: (1) bed front 3/4 product photo, (2) cover texture close-up, (3) tag close-up.
- **Variants at launch:** v1 kitchen tile (above) · v2 visual layer: left = Dana awake in bed, phone at 2:04 a.m., dog silhouette
  pacing in the doorway; right = both asleep.
- **Copy seeds:** P1 "It's 2 a.m. and you can hear his nails on the kitchen floor again." · P2 "He's 12. He used to sprawl on
  his bed. Now he picks the cold tile — and the bed isn't the reason you think." · H1 "Why old dogs leave their beds" · H2
  "100 nights to see him stretch out again". Full copy → `andromeda2-ad-copy`.
- **Proof & compliance:** no health claim; "100-night trial" from the offer sheet [P]. No personal-attribute callout.
- **If it wins:** i1 message — "He's not restless. His bed is flat." (education frame) · i2 visual — a different senior dog/breed
  (golden, shepherd) · i3 format — carousel H1 (tile → cause → Two-Layer Lift → review → offer) · video twin: UGC night-scene
  script with the same angle (`andromeda2-video-scripts`).
- **Score:** side 2 · moment 2 · specific 2 · desire 2 · mechanism 1 · hook 2 · distinct 2 · proof 1 · room 2 · compliance 2 = **18**.
- **Ad names:** `P1-S1_2am-tile-split_v1`, `P1-S1_2am-tile-split_v2`.

### S3 — C03 · A02 Rob-3-beds × Us vs Them — concept

- **Visual direction:** left Nestwell in sage with a large senior dog stretched on it; right a generic grey foam bed, visibly

### S5 — C05 · A01 Dana-2am × Native post (founder) — concept
- **Question:** "Am I the only one up at night with an old dog?"
- **Format:** a real post published on the founder's page, rendered as a 1:1 image and also run as a plain text post. No logo,
  no product shot (the comments will ask).
- **Post text (founder's true story [P — founder confirms every line]):** "Juno is 13. For a year she paced the house every
  night and ended up on the bathroom tiles. I thought it was just age — then I cut her old bed open. What finally got your old
  dog to sleep through?"
- **Why:** Pellegrini's native-post quick win — comments give the side's exact words for the next round; engagement lowers CPM.
- **Compliance:** founder's real experience; no fake comments or seeded profiles; answer real comments from the brand page.
- **9:16:** the post card centred between 15% and 65% on a plain founder-page background.
- **Copy seeds:** primary text = the post itself; H1 "Old dogs, long nights".
- **If it wins:** mine the comments for 5 new main lines; i1 a post-it version of the best comment (E3, with permission).
- **Score:** 2 · 2 · 2 · 1 · 0 · 2 · 2 · 1 · 1 · 2 = **15** (ship: native intent beats the low mechanism score here).
