The root of it

It’s not you. It’s skin after 45.

Three things change, and most foundations ignore all three.

1. Your tone isn’t one colour anymore

Sun spots, redness, a shifted undertone. A fixed pigment matches one of them, so you see patches, a white cast or an orange edge.

2. Thinner, drier skin

Around menopause skin makes less oil and holds less water. Matte formulas pull out what is left, then crack and collect in lines.

3. Formulas built for 25-year-old skin

Heavy pigment, mattifying powders, drying alcohol, fragrance: made to sit on young, oily skin. On yours they emphasise every line.

A shade chart can’t fix that. The foundation has to adapt to you, and be kind to the skin underneath.

Questions women ask before they order

Will it really match my skin tone?

It goes on white and its pigments shift toward your own tone as you blend, so there is no shade to pick. It is made for fair to deep skin, and most buyers write that it matches; a few very fair or very deep tones find it a little light or dark. That is exactly what the 30-day guarantee is for: wear it in your own daylight, and if it isn’t your match, email us for a full refund, even opened.

Will it settle into my fine lines like the others?

It is designed not to: the base is a satin serum, not a matte formula, and soft-focus powders blur the look of lines instead of filling them with pigment. Use one thin layer and skip powder on dry areas. Women in their 50s, 60s and 70s describe it that way in the reviews above.

My skin is very dry. Will it look patchy?

Dry, mature skin is who we made it for. Two forms of hyaluronic acid, ceramide NP, squalane and panthenol keep the finish comfortable, so it is made not to crack or cling to dry patches. Apply it over your usual moisturiser.

How much coverage does it give?

One pump gives an even, natural, medium coverage that softens sun spots and redness. For a darker spot, tap a second thin layer just there. It builds without looking heavy; some buyers wanted more coverage, and said so in their four-star reviews above.

I have sensitive skin. Will it sting?

It is fragrance-free and made without propylene glycol, drying alcohol or parabens, the usual reasons thinner skin stings. As with anything new, try it on your jawline first. It contains salmon-derived DNA (PDRN): not for anyone with a fish allergy.

Does it have SPF?

No. We left SPF out on purpose so the formula can stay a light, hydrating serum. Apply your daily sunscreen first; the foundation layers on top made to layer without pilling.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every pack on this page is a one-time purchase: you pay once and nothing renews. If you want refills later, you can set them up in our store yourself; we never tick that box for you.

How long does a bottle last?

About a month of daily use with one pump a day. The 2-bottle pack is about two months, the 3-bottle pack about three.
