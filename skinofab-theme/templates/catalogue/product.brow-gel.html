1. Prep
Start with clean, dry brows. For best results, make sure your brows and applicator are free from makeup or oils.

2. Lift
Using the applicator, apply a small amount of product and brush through the brows with soft upward strokes to lift and coat every hair.

3. Shape
Use the comb side to sculpt your ideal brow shape, gently guiding the hairs upward and outward for a fuller look.

4. Set
Lightly press and smooth the brows into place to lock in the shape for long-lasting hold.
