Careers

We are seeking a talented and creative Social Media Expert to join our dynamic team. This role is ideal for someone passionate about crafting engaging content, building online communities, and driving meaningful interactions across various social media platforms. As a Social Media Expert, you will play a crucial role in shaping our brand's online presence, enhancing our audience engagement, and contributing to our overall digital marketing strategies.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Content Creation and Management: Develop and implement a content calendar that aligns with our brand's identity, goals, and promotional activities. Craft compelling content for various social media platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, and YouTube, to engage our target audience.
  • Community Engagement: Monitor social media channels for customer queries, comments, and feedback. Actively engage with the community to foster positive experiences and build loyal followers.
  • Analytics and Reporting: Utilize analytics tools to track the performance of social media campaigns and content. Generate reports on metrics such as engagement rates, follower growth, and conversion rates to inform future strategies.
  • Trend Analysis: Stay abreast of the latest social media trends, tools, and technologies. Implement innovative strategies to keep our brand at the forefront of digital marketing developments.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with the marketing team to ensure that social media strategies complement overall marketing efforts. Collaborate with other departments to gather information and stories that can be transformed into compelling social media content.

Requirements:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, or a related field.
  • Proven experience in social media management, content creation, and community engagement.
  • Strong understanding of social media metrics and tools for tracking performance.
  • Excellent copywriting and communication skills, with the ability to adapt tone and style to match our brand’s voice.
  • Creative mindset with a keen eye for detail.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Up-to-date with the latest trends and best practices in social media marketing.

What We Offer:

  • A collaborative and supportive work environment where creativity and innovation are encouraged.
  • Opportunities for professional growth and development.
  • Competitive salary and benefits package.
  • Flexible working hours and the possibility for remote work.

How to Apply:

If you are passionate about social media and eager to contribute to our team, please send your resume, cover letter, and portfolio of social media projects to careers@skinofab.com. In your cover letter, highlight your experience, accomplishments in social media marketing, and why you are a great fit for this role.

We look forward to hearing from you and exploring how you can contribute to our team as a Social Media Expert.
