## Problem mechanism they believe → the real one
- Believe: "there are a few mice; catch them and it's over."
- Real: mice move along **scent highways**, pheromone and urine trails that guide every mouse back through the same entry points. Removing a few mice leaves the trails, so new ones follow them in. Fix the trail and the entry points, not the individual mouse. This is "the Scent Trail Cycle".
