Job 1 · Blocks the nosePeppermint oil · 40%

Menthol is the strongest smell in the blend, and a mouse's nose is far more sensitive than yours. At 40%, the scent around the pouch drowns out everything behind it, food included.

The mouse stops at the pouch and backs away.

Job 2 · Erases the trailCinnamon oil · 5%

Mice mark their route with scent so the next one can follow it in. Cinnamon's sharp, spicy smell sits on top of those marks and covers them.

The next mouse finds no path to follow.

Job 3 · Takes away the nestCedarwood oil · 4%

Mice settle where it's quiet and smells safe: cupboards, corners, storage. Cedarwood's heavy wood scent hangs in exactly those spots.

Nowhere smells safe to settle, so they don't stay.

Job 4 · Keeps it workingCastor-oil base · 5%

Plant oils on their own evaporate fast. The thick castor-oil base holds the blend inside the pouch and lets it out a little at a time.

The barrier stays up for up to 90 days.

Is it OK around my dog, kids or livestock?

There's no poison and no rodenticide in it. The formula is four plant oils: peppermint, cinnamon, cedarwood and castor. As with any household product, place pouches where children and pets can't chew on them.

How many do I need and where do I put them?

A single room, RV or garage needs 1 pack. A full home needs 2–3 packs. A large home with a shed or outbuildings needs 4–6, and a farm or full property 8 or more. Place a pouch every 8–10 feet along entry points: doors, under sinks, behind appliances, basement corners.

Concentration and delivery. Store-bought sprays are usually under 1% peppermint oil and fade within hours. Our pouches hold a 40% peppermint blend in a castor-oil base that releases slowly for up to 90 days.

How long does one pouch last?

Up to 90 days. Replace pouches every 90 days for continuous protection.

Just place it. No setup, no baiting, no checking, no resetting.
