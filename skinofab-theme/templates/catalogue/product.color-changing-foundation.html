Why it happens

Why foundation stops working on skin after 45

It isn't your technique. Three things changed, and most foundations ignore all three.

1

Your tone isn't one color anymore

Sun spots, redness and a sallow, olive or grey cast mean your face now has several tones. One fixed pigment can only match one of them, so you see patches, a white cast or an orange edge.

2

Your skin got thinner and drier

After menopause skin makes less oil and holds less water. Matte and long-wear formulas pull moisture out of it, then crack and collect in every line.

3

Formulas are made for 25-year-olds

Heavy, high-pigment foundations are built to sit on top of young, even, oily skin. On mature skin they cover, but they emphasize every line and dry patch.

The solution

How the Adaptive Peptide Foundation works

A color-adapting foundation built on a serum, so it adjusts to your tone and treats the skin underneath.

1. Color-adapting pigments

Encapsulated pigments go on white. As you blend, they release and mix into your tone within your base, Light–Medium or Medium–Deep. No shade to guess.

2. A hydrating serum base

Two forms of hyaluronic acid, ceramide NP, squalane and panthenol keep dry, mature skin comfortable, so the finish stays satin instead of tight or flaky.

3. PDRN, peptides and Pro-Xylane

Skincare actives for thinner skin after 45, with Matrixyl 3000 peptides, niacinamide and adenosine. Named on the bottle, not hidden in a "complex".

4. Soft-focus powders

Silica and boron nitride scatter light to blur the look of lines and pores, without the dry, matte finish that settles into them.

FAQ

Questions women ask us before they order

Which base should I choose?

Light–Medium suits fair to medium skin with pink or neutral undertones. Medium–Deep suits medium, olive, golden and deeper skin. Between the two, or not sure? Take the 30-second base quiz. And if the base you pick isn't right, we'll swap it for the other one within 30 days.

How does the monthly refill work?

You get one 30 mL bottle in your base every month for $33, shipped automatically. We email you before every refill. Want to switch base, skip a month or cancel? Email hey@skinofab.com: no minimum, no fees, no questions.

Will it settle into my fine lines?

It's designed not to. The base is a satin serum, not a matte formula, and soft-focus powders blur the look of lines instead of filling them with pigment. For the smoothest finish, use one thin layer and skip powder on dry areas.

My skin is very dry. Will it look patchy?

Dry, mature skin is who we made it for. Two forms of hyaluronic acid, ceramide NP, squalane and panthenol keep the finish comfortable, so it doesn't crack or cling to dry patches. Apply it over your usual moisturizer.

Will it cover sun spots and redness?

One pump gives an even, natural, medium coverage that softens sun spots and redness. For a darker spot, tap a second thin layer just there. It builds without looking heavy.

I have sensitive skin. Will it sting?

It's fragrance-free and made without propylene glycol, drying alcohol or parabens, the usual reasons thinner skin stings. As with anything new, try it on your jawline first.

Does it have SPF?

No. We left SPF out on purpose so the formula can stay a light, hydrating serum. Apply your daily sunscreen first; the foundation layers on top without pilling.

How long does it last?

It's made to stay even and comfortable through a full day, without turning orange or drying into your lines. If you're out from morning to dinner, a light touch-up on the nose and chin is usually all it needs.

Can I buy just one bottle?

We sell it as a month-to-month refill, so a fresh bottle arrives before you run out. If it isn't for you, cancel after your first bottle and you're never charged again, and that first bottle is covered by our love it or your money back guarantee.

How does the guarantee work?

Wear it in your own daylight for 30 days. If your base isn't right, email us and we'll send the other base. If you don't love it, we'll refund your first bottle.

When will my order arrive?
