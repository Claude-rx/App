How it started

Born in a tiny kitchen lab.

Born from a simple yet powerful idea — that beauty should be a celebration, not a standard — Smooche began as a small studio with one mission: to redefine how the world sees cosmetics. What started with a single lip gloss formula quickly evolved into a full-range beauty brand, from luscious lip plumpers to advanced brow serums.

Every product is thoughtfully crafted, combining high-performance formulas with skin-loving ingredients — designed for real people and real routines.

Our belief
