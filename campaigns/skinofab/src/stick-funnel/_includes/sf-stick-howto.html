How to use it

Twist, dot, buff

  1. 1. Twist up a little

    A few millimeters is all you need.

  2. 2. Dot it on

    A few dots on your cheeks and forehead. It looks white at first.

  3. 3. Buff it in

    Small circles with the brush end until it shifts to your tone.

  4. 4. Need more? Add a layer

    A little more on spots and redness, then buff the edges.
