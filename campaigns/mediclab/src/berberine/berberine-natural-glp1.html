The root problem

It's not your willpower. After 40, your “I'm full” signal gets quieter.

Every time you eat, your gut releases a hormone called GLP-1. It's your body's own “I'm full, slow down, store less” message: it tells your brain you've had enough and helps insulin clear sugar from your blood after a meal. It's the same hormone the famous injections are built to imitate.

Here's what changes. Research shows the after-meal GLP-1 response tends to be smaller in adults carrying extra weight (Færch et al., Diabetes, 2015). At the same time, with age, less muscle and long hours of sitting, your cells answer insulin more slowly.

  • 4The extra energy gets stored, mostly around the middle

That's why the diet that worked at 32 stops working at 45. Diets fight your hunger with willpower. But hunger isn't the root problem. The signal is. We call it the Quiet Signal.

The root solution

Don't fight the hunger. Give your quiet signal backup at every meal.

If the problem is a quieter after-meal signal, the fix has to happen at meals, every day, not at 9:30 pm with willpower. That's the idea behind the 3-Step GLP-1 Support Routine, built around MedicLab Berberine:

  1. The Capsule. MedicLab Berberine with lunch and dinner. In laboratory research, berberine increased the body's own GLP-1 release (Yu et al., Biochemical Pharmacology, 2010), and human trials study it for healthy glucose metabolism.* It's your body's own signal, supported. Not a synthetic hormone.
  2. The Plate. Protein first, then everything else. Protein-rich meals raise GLP-1 more than carb-heavy ones (Lejeune et al., AJCN, 2006), so you feel full on less.
  3. The Walk. Ten easy minutes after dinner. Working muscle pulls sugar from the blood without waiting for insulin; light walking after meals lowered after-meal blood sugar across seven trials (Buffey et al., Sports Medicine, 2022).

Capsule, plate, walk. Three small moves at the exact moment your body releases GLP-1, so your weight-loss plan finally runs on a system instead of willpower.*
