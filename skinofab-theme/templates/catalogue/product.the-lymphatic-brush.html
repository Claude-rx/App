First time using a Lymphatic Brush from SKINOFAB? This quick video shows the exact steps:

Quick clean, before each use (10 seconds)

  • Spray a brush cleaner or 70% isopropyl alcohol onto a clean cloth
  • Sweep the bristles across it a few times
  • Let it air dry for a minute before using it

Deeper clean, once a month

  • Swirl only the bristle tips on a damp, soapy cloth
  • Rinse them the same way on a clean, wet cloth
  • Dry overnight with the bristles facing down
  • Store it in its case only once fully dry

Avoid

  • Soaking the brush or holding it under running water
  • Wetting the handle
  • Hot water or a hair dryer
